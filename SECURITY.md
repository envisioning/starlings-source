# Security policy

Send vulnerability reports to contact@starlingshq.com with "Security" in the subject. Do not open a public issue.

Include the affected version or commit, the steps to reproduce, and what an attacker gains. We answer as soon as a person has read the report, and we tell you when a fix ships.

## In scope

- The code in this repository: the Worker, the web client, the Mac, iPhone, iPad, Watch and Android apps, and the browser extension.
- The hosted service at `*.starlings.work`, within the limits below.

## Out of scope

- Denial of service, spam and social engineering.
- Reports from automated scanners with no working proof.
- Your own self-hosted deployment's configuration.

When you test the hosted service, use a workspace you own. Do not read, change or keep anyone else's data. Stop and report as soon as you can show the problem.
